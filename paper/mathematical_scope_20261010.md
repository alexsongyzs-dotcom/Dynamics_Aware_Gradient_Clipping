# Mathematical mechanism scope

All proposition statements and full proofs are in the manuscript appendix. Main text retains a concise summary and reference only.

Proved: stationary mean-field reference instability; one-step direction penalty monotonicity and saturation; identical trajectories when the direction penalty is inactive; exact same-run replay equivalence; optimal causal threshold and policy approximation error in an active-clipping quadratic momentum model; exact information gain for nested pre-gradient information sets.

Not proved: universal superiority of DAGC, superiority over independent-calibration replay over full training, incremental alignment benefit in the observed datasets, or improved test generalization. The positive result requires an informative conditional prediction and a policy whose approximation error is smaller than its value. DAGC has not been shown to meet those conditions.

The illustrative quadratic objective is an analytical model; no curvature experiment or curvature-dependent controller is introduced.
